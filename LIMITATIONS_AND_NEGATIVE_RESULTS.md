# Limitations and negative results (S11) - read before citing any SentinelMesh-X number
1. **Detector-independent retrieval fails.** With detector identity and detector-authored wording removed (Track B), DAHR/TF-IDF/BM25/RRF/FlatRAG/TwoLayerRAG score 0% top-1 on the 706- and 227-record corpora (M1).
2. **The 100% lab numbers are detector-aware and in-distribution.** Registry lookup and a supervised classifier also reach 100%; 50% of `raw`, 100% of `title_raw` and 14% of `rewrite` queries contain detector wording. A field-name-only classifier reaches 64%.
3. **Independent data is much weaker.** On 5,935 Atomic events (1,320 tests, 281 techniques) Sigma rules fire on 17%; when they fire the top technique is right 32.6% (CI 28.0-38.0%). Text-only TF-IDF is right 21.7% of all events (it never abstains). Events are command definitions with an inferred Image field, not captured telemetry, and there is no benign data.
4. **Zero-shot is poor.** Unseen techniques: 21% top-1, 79% confidently wrong without abstention, 36% still confidently wrong after calibrated abstention (threshold fitted on development captures did not transfer).
5. **Safety gate removes autonomy.** In every lab scenario the oracle impact tier is analyst/admin, so `auto` is never correct; the gate's 0% auto-on-wrong is therefore partly trivial. Its first-pass under-escalation was 14.7%; I then changed the rule to assume the worst plausible candidate severity when uncertain (4.5%) AFTER seeing test results - that revision is not clean held-out evidence (first-pass log kept: `results_x_v3/m2_first_pass.log`). Over-escalation is 3% and clean alerts raise a trusted-vs-full-view conflict 29% of the time.
6. **Decoy text still flips 14-17% of predictions** (legacy and safe pipelines alike); the gate stops autonomous action, not mislabelling.
7. **Calibration** is measured on perturbations generated from 140 alerts in 5 captures; no independent confidence data. Track B calibration is meaningless (accuracy 0.4%).
8. **Integrity** covers modification after signing only. Poisoned content signed at source verifies. Keys are local-dev Ed25519 with no rotation/revocation/HSM.
9. **Blocked:** pretrained dense / security-domain baselines (no model access); independent annotators (none available); independent environments (none); GPU; commit ids of external data (GitHub API unreachable).
10. The pre-M1 `integrity_check_detection_rate` in `results_x/robustness.json` is unreliable and superseded by `m3_integrity_eval.json`.
